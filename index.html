Injects (G) Gov't spending puts money into the economy → stimulates demand. Deficit: G > T → must borrow → builds public debt

3 Types of Efficiency NBCh 2
  • Allocative — Right mix of goods. Price = marginal cost (P = MC). No one can be made better off by shifting resources.
  • Productive — Lowest-cost production. Economy sits on the PPF — no inputs wasted.
  • Dynamic — Efficient over time. Resources support innovation and investment into the future.

Pareto, Nozick & Welfare Functions NBCh 5
Pareto A policy is OK only if at least one person gains and nobody loses. Rarely met in practice. Three ways redistribution can still be Pareto-compatible: (1) Externality — helping the poor benefits everyone (less crime, disease); (2) Insurance — behind a veil of ignorance, rational people choose social safety nets; (3) Altruism — rich people genuinely gain utility when the poor are better off.
Nozick Libertarian view. Redistribute only to fix injustice. Three principles of justice:
① AcquisitionYou can take unowned things as long as you don't leave others worse off
② TransferVoluntary transfers of justly held goods are always just
③ RectificationUnjust gains or forced transfers must be corrected
Cardinal (Utilitarian)Ordinal
W = UA + UB — utility is measurable and can be added across people W = W(UA, UB) — only rankings, no measurement or comparison needed
Rawls (maximin) W = Min(UA, UB) — social welfare = the worst-off person's utility. To raise W, raise the bottom. Behind the veil of ignorance, rational people choose this rule. Limitation: ignores everyone else — even a tiny gain for the poorest can justify huge sacrifices by everyone else.
Optimal welfare point H: where the utility possibility curve (QR) touches the highest attainable social welfare contour (W curve).

Arrow's Impossibility — 5 Conditions (no rule can satisfy all)
  • Rationality — clear, complete preferences
  • Transitivity — if X>Y and Y>Z then X>Z (else Condorcet paradox)
  • Non-dictatorship — no single person controls all outcomes
  • Pareto — if everyone prefers X, society must prefer X
  • IIA — X vs Y ranking can't be changed by adding a third option Z

Criteria for a Good Tax & Equity NBCh 11
  • Equity — fair; horizontal & vertical
  • Efficiency — minimal distortion & DWL
  • Simplicity — easy to understand and comply with
  • Revenue adequacy — raises enough to fund public spending
  • Flexibility — adjusts automatically to economic conditions; progressive tax = automatic stabiliser (tax falls in recessions, rises in booms)

Vertical equityHigher ability to pay → pay more. Main justification for progressive tax.
Normal vs Excess Burden NBCh 12
Normal (General) BurdenExcess Burden (DWL)
Effect Income effect only — you're poorer but don't switch between goods Income + substitution effect — you switch away from taxed goods
Prices Relative prices unchanged → no distortion Relative prices distorted → DWL triangle appears
Welfare loss Exactly equal to revenue raised Exceeds revenue raised
DWL grows with (1) Tax rate² — doubling the rate more than doubles the DWL    (2) Price elasticity — more elastic market = bigger quantity response = bigger DWL
Graph steps E₀ (P₀, Q₀) → selective tax shifts supply up → E₁ (P₁, Q₁). Revenue = tax × Q₁ (rectangle). DWL = triangle between Q₁ and Q₀ — value lost that gov't never collected.
Tax Neutrality & Lump Sum NBCh 12
Neutral tax No excess burden — welfare loss = revenue exactly. General taxes approach neutrality. Selective taxes are always non-neutral.
Lump sum Fixed amount regardless of behaviour → no substitution effect → no DWL → Pareto-efficient. Like a poll tax — same bill for everyone no matter what you earn or buy.
Why it fails in practice: Regressive — same rand amount hits low earners harder. Ignores ability to pay. Politically unpopular.
Lump Sum vs Selective Tax (Same Revenue) NB Key DiagramCh 12

Bottom line: For the same revenue, a lump sum tax always leaves the consumer better off than a selective tax. The selective tax forces the consumer onto a lower indifference curve than necessary — the extra drop is the excess burden (pure social waste).
